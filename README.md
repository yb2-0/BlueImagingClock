This is a repo for calculating feasibility of blue imaging in our clock setup. More to come.

`Polarizability_and_StarkShift.ipynb` asks how much light from a neighboring 171Yb tweezer array leaks onto the clock array (always in 460.862 nm tweezers), and what that does to the clock atoms.

There are two scenarios we progressively evaluate:

1. Blue OR Green imaging in an imaging array that is 45um away from from the clock zone with a phase mask, but without any scattering cross section calculations (only residual resonant light causing scattering in the clock array.)
2. Blue imaging in 517 or 532 tweezers 450um away from the clock array, where we include the scattering cross section in addition to the residual light.
